# ADR-002: RabbitMQ plus durable outbox/inbox

Status: Accepted; date 2026-10-11; assignment design baseline.

## Context / decision

Command routing and modest volume fit queues. Persist domain+outbox atomically and consume inbox+effects transactionally.

## Alternatives / consequences

Kafka offers retained log/replay but partition/offset complexity is unnecessary core; no exactly-once promise.

## Verification / reconsideration trigger

Kill publisher and consumer at boundaries; preserve stable IDs, ACK after commit.

Implementation may supersede this decision only with a new ADR preserving required learning outcomes and updating contracts, diagrams, task evidence and coverage. No undocumented deviation.
