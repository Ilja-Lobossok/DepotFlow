# ADR-009: Handover fencing

Status: Accepted; date 2026-10-11; assignment design baseline.

## Context / decision

Booking atomically claims handover before irreversible physical transfer; cancellation loses after claim.

## Alternatives / consequences

Claim can strand without physical act; no automatic expiry safe in presence of lost response.

## Verification / reconsideration trigger

Manual review explicitly checks warehouse act and never silently releases possibly rented asset.

Implementation may supersede this decision only with a new ADR preserving required learning outcomes and updating contracts, diagrams, task evidence and coverage. No undocumented deviation.
